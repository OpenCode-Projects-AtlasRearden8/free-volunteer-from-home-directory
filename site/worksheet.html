Pick Your First Project

Answer a few quick questions. No data leaves your browser — this page is a little script that runs locally and suggests a match from this directory, plus a gentle plan for your first week. You can change your answers and re-run as often as you like.

1. How much time can you honestly give this week?

2. Do you want to talk to people?

3. Which sounds more like you this month?

4. Are you 18 or older?

How the worksheet picks

The script scores each of the six directory entries against your answers and shows the best fit with an honest note about why. It is deliberately simple: it applies the same guidance the entries give in plain language — age rules, training time, whether you interact with people — and it is meant to get you started, not to seal your fate. If the first match doesn't feel right, run it again or just pick the second-place suggestion.
